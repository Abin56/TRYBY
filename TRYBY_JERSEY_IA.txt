  SECTION 2 — CATEGORY HIERARCHY
================================================================================

  DESIGN PRINCIPLE:
  Jerseys have three primary discovery dimensions:
    1. SPORT      — Football vs Cricket (the top-level fork)
    2. TYPE       — Club / National / Retro (sub-dimension within sport)
    3. ENTITY     — Specific club or nation (the intent-match layer)

  Every user has a primary intent that fits one of these:
    "I want a [sport] jersey"            → Sport hub
    "I want a [club/nation] jersey"      → Entity page
    "I want a [specific product]"        → Search / PDP
    "I want to browse"                   → Collections

  Taxonomy should serve ALL four intent patterns simultaneously.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  LEVEL 4: PRODUCT
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  Each product (jersey) has these attributes — all filterable:

  REQUIRED:
    sport           Football | Cricket
    type            Club | National | IPL | Retro
    entity          Club or nation slug (FK to entity page)
    season          "2024-25" | "2023-24" | "2022-23" | "Classic"
    kit             Home | Away | Third | Goalkeeper | Special Edition
    name_number     Player name/number on back (Yes/No) + customise option
    sizes           XS | S | M | L | XL | XXL | Kids (4-5Y, 6-7Y, 9-10Y, 12-13Y)
    material        Player Version (ADV) | Fan Version (STD)
    license         Official | Replica | Unofficial (must be clearly labelled)

  OPTIONAL:
    patch           UCL patch | FIFA patch | League patch
    captain_band    Yes/No
    match_worn      Yes/No (premium tier)

  PRODUCT URL PATTERN:
    /jerseys/football/[club-slug]-[season]-[home|away|third]-jersey
    e.g. /jerseys/football/real-madrid-2024-25-home-jersey
         /jerseys/cricket/csk-ipl-2024-home-jersey
         /jerseys/cricket/india-t20-world-cup-2024-jersey

  SECTION 5 — URL STRUCTURE & SEO ARCHITECTURE

  JERSEY SEO IS DRIVEN BY LONG-TAIL INTENT:
  People search:  "manchester united 2024 home jersey india"
                  "india cricket jersey online buy"
                  "csk ipl 2024 jersey"
                  "retro brazil world cup jersey"

  URL STRUCTURE MUST CAPTURE THIS INTENT:

  CATEGORY PAGES (broad intent):
    /jerseys/football                   "football jerseys"
    /jerseys/football/club              "club football jerseys india"
    /jerseys/football/national          "national team football jerseys"
    /jerseys/cricket/ipl                "ipl jersey 2024"

  ENTITY PAGES (mid-tail):
    /clubs/manchester-united            "manchester united jersey india"
    /clubs/csk                          "csk jersey buy online"
    /nations/india                      "india cricket jersey official"

  PRODUCT PAGES (long-tail):
    /jerseys/cricket/india-t20-wc-2024-jersey
    "india t20 world cup jersey 2024 official buy"

  COLLECTION PAGES (event-driven):
    /collections/ipl-2026              "ipl 2026 jerseys"
    /collections/world-cup             "fifa world cup jerseys india"

  SEO RULES:
    1. Every product slug includes: entity + season + kit type + "jersey"
    2. Every category page has unique <h1> and meta description
    3. Club pages use official club name (not abbreviation) for H1
    4. Breadcrumbs on all product pages:
       Home > Football Jerseys > Club Jerseys > Manchester United > [Product Name]
    5. Structured data (JSON-LD): Product schema on all PDPs
       includes: name, brand (TRYBY), offers (price, availability), image
