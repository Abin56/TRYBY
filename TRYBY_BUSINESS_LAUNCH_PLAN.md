## 3. Business Model: POD vs Dropshipping vs Inventory

### Recommendation: Start Pure POD → Hybrid at Month 4

**Phase 1 — Pure POD via Qikink (Months 1–3)**
- Zero inventory risk
- 44–53% gross margin
- Delivery: 4–7 days total
- Your focus: designs, marketing, customer service

**Phase 2 — Hybrid (Month 4+)**
Once 2–3 designs are proven sellers, order 30–50 pieces wholesale from IRA/Noble:
- Cost: ₹180–350/piece → Selling price: ₹699–999 → **Margin: 55–70%**
- Dispatch: 24–48 hours (faster than POD)

**Why NOT pure traditional dropshipping:**
- AliExpress India shipping = 15–25 days → kills conversion
- No white-label branding control
- High RTO due to customer impatience

**Why NOT pure inventory from Day 1:**
- You don't know what sells yet
- Apparel inventory goes obsolete fast
- Cash tied up before validation

## 6. How to Test Before Investing Heavily

### 4-Step Validation (2–3 weeks, ₹0–₹3,000)

**Step 1 — Instagram Story Poll Test (Day 1–3)**
Post jersey mockup (free on Qikink or Canva). Run Stories polls:
- "Would you buy this jersey for ₹699?" → Yes/No
- "Which sport first: Cricket / Football / Both?"

DM everyone who votes Yes — collect WhatsApp numbers.

**Step 2 — WhatsApp Pre-Order Test (Day 3–7)**
Share mockups in 5–10 sports WhatsApp groups:
> *"Launching TRYBY Sports — custom jerseys with your name/number. ₹699 including delivery. React 🔥 if interested."*

**Rule: 10+ interested = proceed. Fewer than 3 = change the design.**

**Step 3 — Zero-Spend COD Test (Day 7–14)**
Launch store, share link to WhatsApp list, accept COD orders. Fulfill 5–10 orders through Qikink. Measure quality, delivery time, and return rate.

**Step 4 — Paid Validation (₹2,000–3,000)**
Run one Instagram Reel ad: India, Age 16–30, Interests: Cricket/Football/Sports. ₹300/day × 7 days.
**If you get 3+ orders from ₹3,000 spend → cost per acquisition under ₹1,000 → scale it.**

## 9. WhatsApp Marketing Strategy

### Tool Recommendation
- **Now:** WhatsApp Business App (free) — sufficient until 500 customers
- **Later:** Upgrade to **Wati, AiSensy, or Interakt** (Indian BSPs, ₹1,999–5,999/month) for unlimited broadcasts

### Setup Checklist
- [ ] Product catalog with all designs, prices, store link
- [ ] Auto-reply: *"Thanks for messaging TRYBY! We reply within 1 hour. View catalog: [link]"*
- [ ] Quick replies for: size chart, delivery time, COD policy, custom order process
- [ ] Away message for non-business hours

### Build Your List
Add everywhere: *"Save our number on WhatsApp to get first access to new drops and exclusive offers."*
Put this in Instagram bio, packaging inserts, website footer.

### Broadcast Rules
- Max 2 broadcasts/week
- Content: new design launches, 48-hour flash sales, match-day offers
- Segments: Interested buyers | Existing customers | Bulk/team buyers

### COD Order Confirmation (Critical — Reduces RTO by 25–35%)
After every COD order, send:
> *"Hi [Name], your TRYBY jersey order (₹699) is confirmed. Reply YES to confirm your delivery address: [address]. We'll dispatch within 48 hours!"*

No reply in 4 hours = call to confirm.

### Abandoned Cart Recovery
- **Message 1** (within 1 hour): *"You left a jersey in your TRYBY cart. Still interested? [link] COD available."*
- **Message 2** (24 hours later): Include ₹50 discount code
- Never send more than 2 recovery messages

### Complete Order Flow via WhatsApp
```
Customer DMs
    → Share catalog
    → Collect: name / jersey details / size / address
    → Send Razorpay payment link
    → Confirm payment → send order ID
    → Dispatch → send Shiprocket tracking link
    → Delivery → "How was your jersey? Photo = ₹50 off next order"
```
